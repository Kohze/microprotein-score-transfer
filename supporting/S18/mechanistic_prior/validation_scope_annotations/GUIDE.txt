S18 Primary validation precedents and citation scope
2 November2026

These annotations support literature claims in the working manuscript. They
do not add predictor outcomes, candidate activity labels or native edited
protein measurements. The CSV and JSON contain the same nine source records:
seven completed scientific reviews and two explicitly partial reviews.
Completed scientific review does not mean replay of every raw assay,
workbook cell, historical pipeline or paper in its citation chain.

Coverage records retain each source's actual scope. Hasenjaeger supplementary
figures were reviewed from original embedded images and its editable S14
alignment; whole Word-page layout was not rendered. No separate Hames2001
SI was identified. Cloutier's main article remains unread, and Chen's detailed
SI remains unread. Those restrictions also qualify the manuscript citations.

Samandi's full scientific figure review was completed after the older S17
bundle was frozen. S18 records the later coverage without rewriting that
earlier artifact. Its original source-definition ambiguities are retained.

PRIMARY_SOURCE_ARCHIVE_MAP.json maps four separate evidence archives by path,
size and SHA256. S17_SOURCE_ARCHIVE_MAP.json retains the earlier cis/screen/
assembly archive mappings, including Cloutier's public supporting material.
The mapped archives are separate files: this small annotation bundle is not
a self-contained copy of all original primary PDFs and workbooks. Verify the
listed hashes when opening those archives. Primary DOIs are recorded per row.

The current68-record ledger,26-claim matrix and scientific-reading queue are
snapshots for traceability. The queue still identifies26 scientific gaps.
These counts are not a certificate of exhaustive literature completion.

The linked reading archives also retain a source-only Ghosh/Chu matched-tail
feasibility check. That prospective contrast is not included as a new Result,
performance claim or validation cohort in this manuscript revision. Its
measurements and overlapping variant ascertainment require separate review.
