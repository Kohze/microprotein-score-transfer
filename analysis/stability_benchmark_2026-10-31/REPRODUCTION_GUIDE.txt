MICROPROTEIN REPRESENTATION TRANSFER BENCHMARK
31 October 2026

Read first
----------
manuscript.pdf is the revised research draft. This directory is an executed
retrospective benchmark, not the earlier feasibility/pilot study. Results are
in results/, including row-level predictions. The original catalogue manuscript
and guide-selection manuscript were not overwritten. The bundled preceding
catalogue package supplies the earlier full annotation/clustering analysis.

The revised paper is an unsubmitted draft for scientific review. No new
endogenous half-life, protein function, biological cluster or mechanism is
established. The independent assay is small and deliberately selected.

name. The endpoint is normalized FLAG-positive fraction, not the Guo score.

Input and design provenance
---------------------------
INPUT_FREEZE.json hashes the pinned source inputs and execution protocol.
EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.txt records the specification frozen before these model fits.
Exploratory pilots predate it; this is not prospective preregistration. A later
code-only correction suppressed cross-scale MAE and added common-coverage
summaries without changing model fitting or choosing a better validation result.
VALIDATION_RELEASE.json records persisted main predictions before validation
scoring in the run. Repeated deterministic runs do not make this blinded.

Sequence verification and historical catalogue reconstruction were performed
in the preceding source audits. Their receipts and scripts are included under
supporting/. Re-running those from genomes requires the cited hg38/GENCODE and
release GTF inputs; the roughly 835 MB hg38 2bit is not duplicated here. The
benchmark itself can be reproduced from the pinned prepared inputs supplied.

Environment and commands
------------------------
The executed analysis used Python 3.11 on Windows, NumPy 1.26.4, pandas 2.0.3,
SciPy 1.11.1, scikit-learn 1.3.0, matplotlib 3.7.2, pyarrow 16.0.0 and
openpyxl 3.0.10. Ridge eigen-solves were checked against sklearn Ridge to
8.4e-15 maximum absolute prediction error. The installed threadpoolctl 2.2.0
is incompatible with this Windows environment; run_benchmark.py used the
preceding vendored threadpoolctl 3.6.0. A fresh environment should install
threadpoolctl 3.6.0. Parasail 1.3.4 was installed into vendor/ and used for
alignments. requirements.txt records these dependencies. No GPU is required.

Existing identical-sequence features and completed alignment edges are reused.
To force those computations, move their cached outputs aside, including their
matching manifest/definition. If changing algorithms or definitions, invalidate
the caches; sequence-list identity alone does not verify a changed algorithm.
The eligible-pair alignment step took about two minutes; full grouped model
fitting took about 21 seconds on this machine. Bootstrap summaries take longer.

Tectonic and a populated TeX cache are needed for build_paper.py. In the original
workspace the portable compiler/cache under tmp/ssrn_tools/ were used. They are
not bundled. Alternatively compile manuscript.tex with a configured LaTeX
installation. Tectonic runs only cached resources and in untrusted mode in this
script. PyMuPDF 1.28.2 and Pillow render PDF previews. The final PDF is included.

Main saved results
------------------
native_GFP_MP.parquet: 1,072 grouped held-out native rows, including all 261
length <=30 cargos. native_MP_mCherry.parquet: 1,046 measured opposite-orientation
rows. native_GFP_MP_mutagenesis.parquet: 1,072 library-specific WT rows.

additional_transfer.parquet: 801 within-study sequences; strict_disjoint flags
602 without a primary development-component connection. Both exact and
operational similarity/gene separation matter. These are not a new publication
or proof that published model authors never inspected their outcomes.

mutation_predictions.parquet: 1,014 exact global Leu-to-Ala and 1,007 exact
DNYNEPKANQ extensions. Native and variant predictions use the same held-out
parent model/calibration. The family-mean baseline and delta-trained models
also exclude test-parent groups. Opposite-family correction excludes those
parents even if they occur in the other family.

Yang_validation.parquet: all 37 separate-study constructs, exact-disjoint 27,
strict-disjoint 20. Direct-model training excludes every Yang-connected group.
No validation outcome enters fitting, direction inversion or calibration.
Primary transfer uses MP-mCherry WT training and FLAG-fusion features; GFP-MP
and native-only are declared sensitivities. A late release-wide audit translated
all 7,152 ShortStop GTF entries and recovered 23 Yang matches, including nine
strict matches, extending the initial Guo-only identifier join. This uses the
same frozen precomputed scores and no predictor fitting. Missing is not zero.

metrics.csv distinguishes raw ranking, calibrated reporter prediction and
cross-assay noncomparable MAE. Mutation raw score differences are retained for
ranking; direct comparison of their raw MAE to reporter-scale models is not
meaningful. common_coverage_metrics.csv compares identical available rows.
native_contrasts.json gives primary paired gains and conditional group intervals.
conditional_intervals.csv gives descriptive secondary intervals, not adjusted
discovery claims. subgroup_summaries.csv retains length/context/complexity
results. ShortStop_threshold_summaries.csv separates calls from continuous rank.
fit_receipts.json records nested penalty choices; calibration_directions.json
records learned isotonic directions. Reference membership, split groups,
alignment edges, feature/null definitions and figure source data are supplied.

secondary_diagnostics.py is explicitly late exploratory work. It reports
top-decile prioritization on common rows (mean reporter score, not function),
baseline/delta coupling, replicate-delta agreement, and 311 specifically verified
additional substitutions across A-to-D, C-to-D, G-to-D, N-to-E, N-to-P and V-to-D.
All changed positions must match the named source/target and other positions
must be preserved; these edits are not assumed to substitute every occurrence.
It applies the fixed WT-model/calibration protocol without selecting a new
primary representation. Small family results are retained, not promoted as
mechanistic discoveries. Verified experimental localization strata are absent
from these pinned inputs and were not invented. See SECONDARY_DIAGNOSTICS_SCOPE.

Verification and interpretation
-------------------------------
results/INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION.json recomputes source replicate means,
PAP coefficients/formula, all primary edits, reference exclusion, group-fold
separation and metrics. PDF review renders every final page and checks bounds,
references and key numeric claims. PACKAGE_MANIFEST.json records file hashes;
PACKAGE_VERIFICATION.json checks the final archive contents. Manuscript numbers
are independently checked against results by finalize_package.py.

The original thesis clustering is NOT exactly reproduced. The new controlled
PCA memberships and two t-SNE maps are computationally repeatable with identical
inputs/settings; broad structure is weakly separated and largely survives
composition-preserving shuffling in family-weighted PCA. UMAP was not rerun,
and functional subclasses were not verified. See supporting/ for the exact
clustering receipt and note, and the preceding complete package for rerun code.

Study limits: outcome-informed retrospective design; aggregate score provenance;
incomplete historical training/pretraining audit; operational sequence grouping;
conditional bootstrap intervals; and a small, selected, construct-shifted
independent panel. No new experimental function or endogenous turnover data.
Publication value is a candidate reproducible benchmark/limitations result,
not an established biological discovery or assurance of acceptance.
