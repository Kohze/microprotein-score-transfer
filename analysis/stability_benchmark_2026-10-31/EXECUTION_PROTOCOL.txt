RETROSPECTIVE MICROPROTEIN BENCHMARK EXECUTION SPECIFICATION
31 October 2026

This executes PUBLICATION_ANALYSIS_PLAN.txt after the exploratory research
pilots. It is retrospective and outcome-informed, not a preregistration or a
blinded confirmatory experiment. All results, including contrary ones, will be
retained. The existing catalogue paper and exploratory research files stay intact.

Targets and provenance
Use authors' released normalized reporter scores, not endogenous half-life,
thermodynamic stability or function. Original FACS/sequencing normalization will
not be reconstructed from aggregate values. Full STAR Methods is currently
unavailable through the accessible publisher endpoints; document the available
supplement, biological-replicate definitions, synthesized oligos, score direction
and code. Rank and within-library score analyses do not require assuming a new
normalization formula. Unavailable raw-depth/censoring metadata limits inference.

The primary development cohort is original Guo GFP-MP WT, with mean scores and
available biological replicates. Keep all valid amino-acid lengths. Report missing
replicates and complete-replicate sensitivity. Treat new 730 HLA-associated and
71 digest-MS-associated sequences as additional within-study cohorts, not new
studies. Evaluate deposited Guo ESM scores there only; distinguish released-
training-disjointness from prospective blinding. Do not compare its in-sample
native scores against other methods' held-out performance as a model contest.

Use same-library WT for mutation effects, primary global Leu-to-Ala and exact
DNYNEPKANQ extension. Flag WT identifiers with inconsistent native sequences
across libraries and exclude their affected comparisons from primary inference.
Same sequence/parent/gene/overlap groups stay together. All engineered variants
inherit parental groups; exact cross-parent construct identity adds a link.

Sequence-similarity grouping
Audit every pair of native assay/validation peptides, reference-classification
sequences and available ShortStop positive-reference counterparts that can meet
bidirectional coverage. Smith-Waterman BLOSUM62, gap-open 10, gap-extension 1;
traceback identity counts exact matches over alignment columns, coverage counts
non-gap residues over each full sequence. Primary links require >=60% identity
and >=80% coverage in BOTH sequences. Sensitivity links require >=40% identity
and the same coverage. Exact identities and shared gene/overlap groups are linked
regardless of alignment. These are operational similarity groups, not proof of
absence of evolutionary homology or pretraining contamination. Record low
complexity and group sizes, including any large component.

Annotation-trained score
Refit the original fixed AAC ExtraTrees classifier after removing all reference
pairs/components connected to any Guo or Yang assay parent, including similarity
links. Use 200 trees, depth 10, leaf minimum 10, sqrt features, balanced classes,
seed 100; report seeds 101/102 sensitivity. Higher output denotes canonical
resemblance. This is neither a stability probability nor a functional label.

Experimental endpoint baselines
Use grouped nested cross-validation in development WT: five outer folds (seed
20261031), three inner group folds and Ridge alpha {0.1,1,10,100,1000} selected
by inner MAE. Standardize using training data only. Models:
L: log length and its square.
A: L + 20 amino-acid frequencies.
T: A + one-hot first five/last five residues, whole/N10/C10 hydropathy and
   published PAP human30 composition/terminal terms.
O: T + eight standardized AAindex property lag-1..5 product descriptors,
   centered/scaled against 199 independent within-peptide shuffles preserving
   the first and last five residues and exact composition. Zero null variance
   produces a zero residual, not a fabricated measured feature. Record null SD.
   Raw low-lag descriptors are a declared sensitivity. No lag-30 imputation.
   Three independently shuffled cargo controls use the SAME composition and
   fixed terminal residues; refit T plus their standardized order descriptors
   on the same folds. Native O must also be compared with these controls before
   attributing any improvement to residue order rather than nonlinear composition
   or finite-sample feature distributions. Shuffled peptides have no measured
   outcome; this is a representation control, not experimental negative labels.
Also run fixed ExtraTrees-regression sensitivity for A/T/O (200 trees, depth 10,
leaf 10, feature fraction 0.7); do not select whichever model wins the outer test.

Frozen external predictors
ShortStop standard/consensus scores are matched by sequence-verified GTF IDs.
Keep continuous ranking, published cutoffs and top-candidate enrichment distinct.
PAP human30 C-terminal min(30,L) window is the primary transfer, scaled according
to the published formula; full-peptide normalized composition is secondary.
Published historical positive sequences are not fully archived; audit available
counterparts and state that limitation. Guo ESM scores are secondary frozen
comparators on released-training-disjoint sequence/group subsets. PepScore is
included in the independent assay where authors supply exact construct scores;
do not invent missing domain, PhyloCSF or transcript-null values elsewhere.

Calibration, comparisons and uncertainty
Primary native contrast: O versus T Spearman and MAE on common held-out rows.
No formal equivalence claim. Report estimates and 95% group-bootstrap intervals
(1000 draws) conditional on predictions, plus declared seed/grouping sensitivities.
For practical scale context, report median absolute replicate disagreement and
half that value as a descriptive MAE resolution, not a biological cutoff.
Secondary contrasts use BH adjustment within each prespecified endpoint family.
Report short (<=30), >=31, ORF context and low-complexity summaries without
choosing favorable subgroups. Missing predictors reduce coverage, never become 0.

For fixed-score calibration, fit a training-WT-only isotonic map inside each
parent/group outer split. Learn orientation from training correlation; report
when the mapping reverses an annotation score rather than concealing it.
For mutation effects, compare raw and calibrated frozen-score differences and
direct endpoint models; native/mutant predictions use the SAME held-out parent
model/calibration. Include baseline score as a descriptive coupling diagnostic
and replicate-difference analyses. Do not infer a mechanism from delta correlation.
Assess cross-family transfer with any mutation-trained correction explicitly
secondary; no model is allowed to train on its held-out parent's mutant outcomes.

Independent validation
Freeze the 37 Yang constructs before scoring them. Primary transfer is the
sequence-disjoint subset after excluding both exact and operational-similarity
connections to Guo development/extra cohorts; also report all 37 and exact-only
27 for coverage. Interpret the outcome as normalized FLAG-positive cell fraction.
Use actual C-terminal FLAG-fusion sequence for construct-aware predictions and
report native-only sensitivity. Do not calibrate to the 37 validation outcomes.
For direct models, train on the appropriate Guo orientation as a prespecified
context sensitivity; label the assay/scale shift. PepScore is evaluated as the
authors' frozen supplied baseline, with its own selection history acknowledged.
Do not make a strong general claim from this small cohort's point estimate.

Brain data is optional supporting evidence only. ShortStop-dependent selection
cannot independently validate ShortStop; no new AD biology is claimed.

Implementation choices frozen before model fitting
Primary independent transfer trains on MP-mCherry WT (free cargo N terminus,
masked C terminus, closer to the FLAG constructs); GFP-MP is the alternate
orientation. Absolute cross-assay errors are not compared. Secondary mutation-
trained Ridge uses changes in A/T/O features and grouped nested validation;
cross-family transfer trains on the opposite family after excluding every
held-out test-parent group. A training-family mean and zero change are baselines.
Fold groups are assigned greedily by descending group size after seeded random
tie breaking, without labels. All reported bootstrap intervals are conditional
on fitted predictions, not full retraining intervals. Primary MAE differences
are error reductions (T minus O); rank differences are O minus T.

Deliverables
Pinned inputs and manifests; exact joins; group/split assignments and similarity
edges; feature definitions; held-out/transfer/variant predictions; calibrated
comparisons and uncertainty; independent assay results; figures with source
data; revised manuscript PDF and editable source; reproducible review package;
numerical/model/provenance audits and visual review of every PDF page.
