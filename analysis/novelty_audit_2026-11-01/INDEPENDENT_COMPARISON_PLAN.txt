INDEPENDENT PEPTIDE-REPORTER TRANSFER TEST - DESIGN BEFORE NEW ASSOCIATIONS
1 November 2026

Purpose and limits
Test the remaining representation-transport hypothesis using an independent
published assay. Voutsinos measures canonical cytosolic 30-residue tiles fused
to GFP; this is an independent experimental source, NOT an independent
full-length nORF cohort, endogenous abundance measurement or function test.
It can assess transport of nORF-trained reporter models to a specified peptide
reporter system. It cannot alone validate broad biological claims about nORFs.

Selection already informed by prior Guo findings
The new source was selected after seeing Guo A/T transfer results and reading
related literature. The analysis is therefore an outcome-informed research
extension. No association between new target measurements and our predictions
has yet been evaluated. Source quality/missingness and membership metadata
may be inspected before fitting; target effect values may not select models.

Source and membership
Use the pinned official repository commit and original author test membership.
The release model_data.out independently confirms 15,818 test tiles, matching
the actual metadata and scores. The paper text says 15,815. Retain all valid
released test members, document the discrepancy, and do not choose three rows
to discard to force the paper count. Require standard 30-mer sequence and a
measured abundance target. Training/test exact peptide overlap is zero.
Exclude genes shared with retrieved Guo and annotation-reference inputs,
then apply the existing >=60% identity and >=80% coverage in each peptide
criterion, with the existing 40% graph as sensitivity. Do not change thresholds
after looking at transfer performance. Retain row-level exclusion reasons.
The original author protein/tile split provides additional PAP training
separation; record the limits of operational homology control.

Primary models and contrast
Train A and T exactly as defined in the existing benchmark, separately on
original GFP-MP WTs and MP-mCherry WTs. Use training-only scaling and nested
group-selected Ridge penalties with the existing seeds/settings. Fit on the
full allowed source training set for external predictions. Save predictions,
hyperparameters, memberships and hashes before joining new target scores.
Do not select a source orientation or reverse a prediction direction based
on target results. Report both source configurations.
Primary external contrast: A-minus-T Spearman correlation against the released
abundance_score on the same eligible rows, with gene-cluster bootstrap
uncertainty conditional on saved predictions. Also show all individual
correlations. Do not compare raw absolute errors across incompatible scales.
Repeat with the stricter similarity criterion and existing second seed.

Necessary diagnostic controls before a strong feature claim
The current T includes many positional features and pretrained PAP terms.
Add separately specified, dimension-aware representations for the source's
exposed terminus, the opposite terminus, and interior positions. Keep tuning
inside source group folds and include training-context performance. Compare
PAP internal versus terminal components as frozen explanatory baselines on
its published held-out data. Add published QCDPred/human25 and relevant
N-/C-specific models where their training/implementation can be audited.
Declare these as diagnostics and retain all outcomes. A nominal A/T contrast
alone does not isolate a terminal mechanism or a benefit of a new method.

What would support the candidate gap
The specific transfer pattern repeats in independent measured data and remains
after dependence, correct-terminus and model-complexity controls. A practical
candidate-selection consequence is demonstrated on the same covered rows.

What would defeat or narrow it
No independent repetition; dependence on a selected source/model; disappearance
with complexity controls; or a result explained by incorrectly exposed-terminal
features. Report that outcome and revise the paper to a narrower benchmark
contribution. Do not turn a failure into a new mechanism by changing endpoints.

Remaining pre-fit verification
Finish the live separation audit, inspect terminal completion, verify source
membership/history and input hashes, and independently check feature extraction
against the existing saved Guo features. Then freeze exact comparison inputs.
The broader related-publication and citation-chain audit continues in parallel
within this research task; source retrieval alone is not completed reading.
