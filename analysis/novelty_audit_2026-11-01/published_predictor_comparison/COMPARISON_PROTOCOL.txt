PUBLISHED COMPOSITION BASELINES - SECONDARY NOVELTY CHALLENGE
1 November 2026

Motivation
QCDPred and its companion paper already demonstrate composition-based degron
prediction and transfer from yeast to human terminal-peptide reporters. The
strong nORF-trained A transfer cannot establish novelty without this baseline.
This comparison is specified after the A/T target results were inspected.
It is not a blinded new-cohort experiment, preregistration or primary analysis.
Keep all specified outputs even if they defeat the publication narrative.

Frozen models
1. Original QCDPred (Johansson et al., JMB 2023, 10.1016/j.jmb.2022.167915):
   published 20 coefficients and intercept; no retraining or calibration.
   Primary peptide score: one minus the arithmetic mean degron probability
   over every consecutive complete 17-mer (the original predictor's windows).
   This direction follows the published endpoint; higher means more stable.
   Sequences shorter than 17 are missing, not padded or extrapolated.
   Secondary representation: one minus the sigmoid of the length-normalized
   complete-cargo composition, using the same coefficients and 17/L factor.
   This is the later PAP implementation's whole-cargo option, not the original
   QCDPred full-protein output. Both results must be retained, without choosing
   an aggregation based on the target outcome.
2. PAP human25: a target-study-trained composition reference. The Voutsinos
   model methods train it on the first 25 residues, excluding the final five.
   Report one-minus-degron probability on the first 25 residues as its target
   internal-region reference, and separately the whole-cargo normalized
   sequence transfer. This shares training provenance with the target study;
   do not call it a wholly external predictor or a fair universal leaderboard.

Implementation verification
Read and hash original QCDPred Python/R source and training table. Extract
only literal numeric parameters from the pinned source via AST, never execute
model serialization. Compare independently calculated 17-mer predictions to
the original official CLI and check coefficients against the yeast17 block
already present in pinned PAP. Document score aggregation and eligible rows.
For human25 use literal values from pinned PAP; no untrusted pickle loading.

Training provenance
QCDPred training is yeast 17-mers from the published peptide screen, with
>50 reads and extreme stable/unstable PSI labels. Audit exact target 17-mer
window matches against the actual labeled training rows. A no-exact-match
result is not homology independence. Cross-species homologs, partial matches
and overlapping training windows may remain. Do not reuse the current
source-separation certificate as proof of QCDPred homology separation.

Evaluation
Use the existing 14,143 eligible author-held-out canonical tiles; repeat on
13,730 under the existing stricter similarity sensitivity. Do not change
membership for a favorable result. Evaluate original GFP-MP and MP-mCherry
WTs separately as the source-domain context, and the existing strict602
additional subset if its saved membership can be verified without changes.
Use Spearman correlation; higher abundance/stability is the reporting direction.
Use no target-dependent inversion, head fitting or outcome-based source choice.
Compare frozen QCDPred to both source-trained A/T predictions on identical
covered target rows. Show selected mean assay score under the existing top
ceil(10%) rule, ties lexical sequence. Conditional gene-bootstrap paired
intervals are secondary, not multiplicity-adjusted discoveries or retraining
uncertainty. Distinguish learned nORF-head transfer from a generic frozen
composition model and a target-study-trained reference.

Interpretation gate
If QCDPred matches the nORF-trained composition heads, portable composition is
a replication/extension of known biology rather than our novel predictor.
A deficit alone does not establish a new mechanism or general superiority.
The remaining claim must concern a specified transfer/decision boundary and
survive appropriate closer precedents, construct semantics and independent
intervention validation. Native nORF function remains a separate endpoint.
